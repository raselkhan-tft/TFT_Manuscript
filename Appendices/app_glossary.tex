\chapter*{Glossary of TFT Terminology}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\protect\numberline{}Glossary of TFT Terminology}
\label{app:glossary}

This appendix provides formal definitions for the specialized terminology and mechanical concepts unique to Tension Field Theory (TFT).

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{description}
\setlength{\itemsep}{0.4cm}

\item[Absolute Lattice Shear Coupling ($K_{\mathrm L}$):] The mechanical coupling coefficient relating the local action scale $\hbar c_{\mathrm E}$ of the Soft Floor surface state to the macroscopic electrostatic combination $k_{\mathrm e}e^2$. It provides the dimensional bridge between the lattice shear topology and the observed electromagnetic coupling scale.

\item[Acoustic Transparency:] The deterministic TFT mechanism proposed for quantum tunneling. It occurs when a coherent standing-wave bridge phase-locks with a geometric barrier so that the effective boundary impedance is suppressed, permitting transport of the localized topological state through the barrier.

\item[Active Vacuum (State I / Soft Floor):] The dynamically active, dual-phase vacuum formed by a Face--Centered Cubic (FCC) nodal scaffold and its connected Interstitial Condensate. The FCC scaffold supplies finite transverse restoring action and surface inertia, while the condensate supplies pressure response, conservative defect-induced added inertia, and the receiving degrees of freedom required for separately activated dissipative processes.

\item[Bipolar Strain Field:] The locked structural strain configuration extending between a Torsional Anchor (nucleus) and a Resonant Standing-Wave Boundary (electron shell). Its macroscopic projection provides the TFT mechanical representation of electrostatic charge structure.

\item[Capacity Surface ($r_{\mathrm{cap}}$):] The mechanical co-motion surface around a rotating TFT black hole at which the torsional drift speed of the Soft Floor equals the local propagation capacity $c_{\mathrm P}$. It serves as the TFT analogue of the Kerr ergosurface and is defined through local wave kinematics and torsional flow.

\item[Catastrophic Decompression (The Big Rupture):] The macroscopic martensitic phase-reset event in which a globally connected State-II saturated structure loses containment and rapidly converts back toward the active FCC configuration, restoring interstitial freedom and initiating the present active cosmic phase.

\item[Center-of-Mass Motion (Boundary Translation):] One of the four velocity channels of a live TFT black hole. It describes translation of the complete saturated FCC/SC phase domain through the surrounding Soft Floor, distinct from phase-front growth, tangential redistribution, and torsional boundary motion.

\item[Chiral Nucleation:] The TFT mechanism proposed for baryon asymmetry in which a globally torsionally biased decompression state favors one handedness of topological defect formation over the opposite handedness, producing an asymmetric matter--antimatter nucleation probability.

\item[CMB Noise Floor:] The statistically isotropic thermal electromagnetic background established by the present Fossil CMB. It is the ambient radiative baseline against which a progressively relaxed coherent photon excitation eventually loses distinct observational identity.

\item[Coherent-Photon Energy-Relaxation Rate ($\Gamma_{\mathrm D}$):] The fractional energy-relaxation rate of the coherent photon eigenbranch in the Active Vacuum, characterizing the phase-lagged dissipative response through which coherent photon energy is transferred into finite-energy FCC--Condensate receiving degrees of freedom; in the weak-loss regime, $\Gamma_{\mathrm D}\simeq\omega\tan\Delta\varphi$, and when the coherent modal action $E/\omega$ is preserved, the same rate governs the real-frequency relaxation, $\dot E_\gamma/E_\gamma=\dot\omega_\gamma/\omega_\gamma=-\Gamma_{\mathrm D}$, thereby generating the accumulated propagation-relaxation redshift; the observational reference scale is set by the low-redshift Hubble slope, while the microscopic scale-transfer construction provides the unit-reference value $\Gamma_{\mathrm D}^{(0)}\simeq2.36\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$.

\item[Completed Coordinate Map ($u_i=\mathcal{C}_i y_i$):] The work-conjugate constitutive constraint derived from the active-capacity balance. It states that the completed displacement $u_i$ accepted by the active FCC contact state is the residual-capacity fraction $\mathcal{C}_i$ of the latent node--condensate reconfiguration $y_i$ presented to the endpoint.

\item[Contact-Point Relative Displacement ($\Delta\mathbf{u}_{ij}^{\mathrm c}$):] The objective local deformation measure entering the nearest-neighbor FCC contact energy. It combines nodal translations, nodal microrotations, and internal phase differences so that the contact law remains frame-indifferent under rigid translation and uniform rotation.

\item[Convergence Information Limit ($N_{\mathrm{QG}}$):] The geometric information capacity assigned to a fully saturated SC-core boundary. It represents the maximum number of substrate longitudinal-strain records available to the corresponding saturated configuration and is distinct from the actual matter-information footprint carried by the system.

\item[Cosmic Critical Mass ($M_{\mathrm U}$):] The total mass assigned to the cosmological domain of characteristic radius $R_{\mathrm U}$,
\[
    M_{\mathrm U}=\int_0^{R_{\mathrm U}}\mathrm{d}m.
\]
In the Type-II saturation construction its distributed surface potential satisfies
\[
    \Phi_{\tau,\mathrm{G,U}}(R_{\mathrm U})
    =
    -\frac{G}{R_{\mathrm U}}
    \int_0^{R_{\mathrm U}}\mathrm{d}m
    =
    -\frac{GM_{\mathrm U}}{R_{\mathrm U}}
    =
    -c_{\mathrm E}^2.
\]
$M_{\mathrm U}$ characterizes the $R_{\mathrm U}$ cosmological domain and remains conceptually distinct from the black-hole saturation mass $M_{\mathrm{Sat}}$.

\item[Cosmic Damping Horizon ($R_{\mathrm U}$):] The cosmological designation for the Photon Energy-Relaxation Length. It is the characteristic macroscopic distance over which the surviving coherent photon branch undergoes one exponential energy-relaxation e-fold. At $R_{\mathrm U}$ the coherent photon retains $1/e$ of its emitted energy and continues propagating with further relaxation beyond that scale.

\item[Cosmic Impedance ($a_{\mathrm D}$):] The macroscopic structural-impedance acceleration scale of the Active Vacuum,
\[
    a_{\mathrm D}
    =
    \frac{c_{\mathrm E}^2}{R_{\mathrm U}}
    =
    c_{\mathrm E}\Gamma_{\mathrm D},
\]
for a matched $(R_{\mathrm U},\Gamma_{\mathrm D})$ pair. It establishes the horizon-scale impedance normalization from which the reduced galactic response scale $a_{\mathrm{gal}}=a_{\mathrm D}/(2\pi)$ is constructed.

\item[Directional-Tension Redistribution:] The mechanical origin assigned to gravitational radiation in TFT. It is a propagating, self-equilibrated transverse-traceless redistribution of directional transfer capacity in the active Soft Floor rather than an ordinary affine volumetric deformation of the FCC scaffold.

\item[Dynamic Covariant Conservation:] The on-shell requirement that the completed effective stress-energy tensor of the coupled TFT system satisfies
\[
    \nabla_\mu^{(\tau)}T_{\mathrm{TFT}}^{\mu\nu}=0,
\]
ensuring compatibility between the dual-phase material balance and the emergent gravitational tensor description.

\item[Effective Photon Energy-Relaxation Rate ($\Gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}$):] The combined effective rate used when coherent-photon vacuum relaxation and localized defect-induced damping are simultaneously retained in the same comparator equation,
\[
    \Gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}
    =
    \Gamma_{\mathrm D}
    +
    \Gamma(x).
\]
It is a composite transport coefficient rather than an additional fundamental vacuum constant.

\item[Exact Capacity Ledger ($T_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}=T_{\mu\nu}^{(\mathrm{rem})}+\Delta T_{\mu\nu}^{(\tau)}$):] The mechanical accounting identity of the State-I tensor budget. It separates the reference structural-capacity tensor $T_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}$ into the remaining active capacity $T_{\mu\nu}^{(\mathrm{rem})}$ and the converted capacity $\Delta T_{\mu\nu}^{(\tau)}$ responsible for localized loading and induced gravitational strain.

\item[Face--Centered Cubic (FCC) Lattice:] The TFT-selected close-packed geometry of the State-I Active Vacuum. Equal spherical nodes occupy the fraction
\[
    \beta_{\mathrm{geo}}
    =
    \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}}
    \approx0.74048,
\]
leaving the connected interstitial fraction $\beta_{\mathrm{IC}}\approx0.25952$. FCC is selected within the close-packed family through cubic symmetry, twelve-neighbor coordination, the retained contact-isotropy closure, and compatibility with the saturated phase.

\item[FCC Node Radius ($r_{\mathrm D}$):] The calibrated physical radius of an Active-Vacuum FCC node,
\[
    r_{\mathrm D}\simeq7.4504\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{m}.
\]
It defines the characteristic active-vacuum structural scale and the microscopic resolution entering the nearest-neighbor spacing $d_{\mathrm{nn}}=2r_{\mathrm D}$ and the photon structural cutoff.

\item[Fossil CMB:] The TFT interpretation of the Cosmic Microwave Background as relic thermal electromagnetic radiation originating from the catastrophic decompression and early annealing epoch and subsequently evolving through the Active Vacuum over cosmic time. Its present radiation field establishes the CMB Noise Floor.

\item[Geometric Capacity ($S_{\mathrm{geo}}$):] The maximum number of structural boundary slots available on a macroscopic saturated phase boundary for physical defect states. For a one-node-thick boundary construction, the geometric capacity scales with surface area as $R^2$.

\item[Global Phase Avalanche:] The aggregate conversion of active State-I vacuum into State-II saturated structure through the global black-hole network. Its instantaneous fractional conversion rate is
\[
    \Gamma_{\mathrm{pc}}

    \frac{\dot M_p}{M_p}.

For a stationary $\Gamma_{\mathrm{pc}}$, the converted mass evolves as $M_p(t)=M_p(0)e^{\Gamma_{\mathrm{pc}}t}$. The phase-conversion rate $\Gamma_{\mathrm{pc}}$ and the photon energy-relaxation rate $\Gamma_{\mathrm D}$ are independent quantities unless an explicit synchronization condition is imposed.

\item[Global Rupture Trigger ($M_{\mathrm{trg}}$):] The aggregate State-II mass threshold associated with the global percolation fraction $\beta_{\mathrm{rupture}}\approx0.0516$. At this threshold, the distributed network of saturated defects forms a spanning cluster across the structural reservoir and initiates Catastrophic Decompression.

\item[Goalpost Topology:] The TFT structural model of quantum entanglement in which separated Soft-Floor states remain phase-correlated through vertical Tension Columns connected to a shared Residual Phase-Tether on the phase-locked Hard Floor.

\item[Gravitational Saturation Radius ($R_{\mathrm{sat}}$):] The spatial boundary at which the active gravitational strain reaches
\[
    \alpha_{\mathrm G}=1,
\]
exhausting the remaining State-I transfer capacity and initiating the FCC-to-SC phase transition. In the black-hole construction this saturation surface coincides with the phase-transition radius $R_{\mathrm{QG}}$.

\item[Graviton (Dynamic Strain Cursor):] The TFT representation of the localized longitudinal substrate strain record associated with a changing Soft-Floor stress configuration. It functions as a dynamic structural cursor within the vertical topology rather than as an independent elementary exchange particle.

\item[Hard Floor (State 0 / Latent Substrate):] The phase-locked Simple Cubic substrate at the irreducible structural scale $\ell_{\mathrm S}\sim10^{-35}\ \mathrm{m}$. It supplies the absolute structural reference, static longitudinal information record, and saturation boundary of TFT. In the singular constraint limit $\mu_{\mathrm S}\rightarrow0$, $K_{\mathrm S}\rightarrow\infty$, and $c_{\mathrm S}\rightarrow\infty$, longitudinal constraint equilibration becomes effectively zero-delay relative to Active-Vacuum dynamics.

\item[Hydrodynamic Impedance:] The conservative kinetic reaction generated when a localized topological defect induces relative motion of the Interstitial Condensate. The reversible energy stored in this flow produces the defect's added inertial loading; dissipative resistance arises only when a separate phase-lagged response is present.

\item[Incrementally Incompressible Coherent Branch:] The reduced Active-Vacuum mode in which the Interstitial Condensate is entrained with the FCC scaffold,
\[
    \mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{rel}}=\mathbf{0},
\]
while incremental volumetric fluctuations satisfy
\[
    \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0.
\]

\item[Interstitial Condensate (volume fraction $\beta_{\mathrm{IC}}$):] The connected fluid-like phase occupying the complementary FCC interstitial fraction

    =
    1-\beta_{\mathrm{geo}}
    \approx0.25952.

Dissipative energy transfer belongs to separately defined phase-lagged response channels.

\item[ISCO Map ($r_{\mathrm{ISCO}}(a_\ast)$):] The Innermost Stable Circular Orbit obtained from the massive-particle Soft-Floor Hamiltonian of the rotating TFT exterior. It supplies the observational mapping between the spin parameter $a_\ast$ and the inner stable-orbit radius.

\item[Kepler Conjecture Limit ($\beta_{\mathrm{geo}}$):] The maximum equal-sphere packing fraction in three-dimensional Euclidean space,

In the retained one-node-thick boundary construction, this occupancy scales linearly with radius, $S_{\mathrm{kin}}\propto R$.

\item[Kinematic State Equation ($\Gamma_{\mathrm D}^2=\frac{4\pi G}{3}\rho_{\mathrm c}$):] The Type-II cosmological state relation obtained by combining a homogeneous spherical mass distribution with the matched relaxation scale $R_{\mathrm U}=c_{\mathrm E}/\Gamma_{\mathrm D}$ and the saturation condition $GM_{\mathrm U}/R_{\mathrm U}=c_{\mathrm E}^2$.
