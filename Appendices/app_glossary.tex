This appendix provides formal definitions for the specialized terminology and mechanical concepts unique to Tension Field Theory (TFT).

\vspace{0.5cm}

\begin{description}
\setlength{\itemsep}{0.4cm} % Adds breathing room between definitions

\item[Active Vacuum (Soft Floor / State I):] The observable Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) lattice emerging from the substrate at the Duality Radius ($r_D \approx 10^{-26} \text{ m}$). Its dual-phase architecture (solid nodes and interstitial condensate) enables transverse wave propagation (light) and hydrodynamic inertia (mass).

\item[Bipolar Strain Field:] The locked, high-tension structural web connecting a central Torsional Anchor (nucleus) to a Resonant Standing Wave Boundary (electron shell). Its macroscopic geometric projection replaces the abstract concept of intrinsic electrostatic charge.

\item[Catastrophic Decompression (The Big Rupture):] The martensitic (diffusionless) phase transition in which a saturated Simple Cubic (SC) core violently snaps outward to restore the Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) geometry. This mechanical rupture replaces the geometric Big Bang singularity.

\item[Event Horizon ($R_{\text{QG}}$):] The physical, crystallographic phase boundary where the active FCC vacuum structurally saturates ($\alpha_G = 1$) and transitions into the static SC Substrate, mechanically arresting vibration and the flow of time.

\item[Goalpost Topology:] The vertical structural framework governing quantum entanglement. Particles on the dynamic Soft Floor remain instantaneously connected via longitudinal Tension Columns anchored to a shared Residual Phase-Tether running along the hyper-rigid Hard Floor.

\item[Interstitial Condensate:] The fluid-like capacity occupying approximately $26\%$ of the FCC active vacuum. It provides the hydrodynamic resistance that generates microscopic inertial mass and the macroscopic inductive reactance observationally attributed to Dark Matter.

\item[Latent Substrate (Hard Floor / State 0):] The absolute foundational reference frame of reality. It is a perfectly rigid, time-frozen Simple Cubic (SC) crystal operating at the fundamental pixel limit ($\ell_S \approx 10^{-35} \text{ m}$).

\item[Lattice Impedance Acceleration ($a_D$):] The fundamental material resistance of the active vacuum ($a_D = c_E \cdot \Gamma_D$). This kinematic drag mathematically replaces Dark Matter halos by establishing a minimum inductive reactance for orbiting baryonic mass.

\item[Photonic Extrusion:] The deterministic geometric process in which a sudden volumetric contraction of an atomic Bipolar Strain Field forces the hyper-rigid lattice to squeeze out a discrete, braided package of neutralized structural tension (a photon).

\item[Spacetime Gravitational Floor (SGF):] The overarching, dual-phase visco-elastic continuum that mechanically replaces the continuous, empty mathematical manifold of General Relativity.

\item[Thermodynamic Impedance Signature:] The localized thermal deficits (observed as $\pm 30\,\mu\text{K}$ CMB anisotropies) resulting from the elevated visco-elastic friction optical waves experience when traversing fossilized acoustic density rings.

\item[Topological Phase Defect (Matter):] A self-sustaining, locked vorticity loop within the contiguous active vacuum. It generates inertial mass exclusively through hydrodynamic drag against the surrounding interstitial condensate.

\item[Universal Torsional Invariant ($J_i$):] The conserved intrinsic torsional resistance of the tension field ($J_i = \rho_i r_i^4$). The Strong, Weak, Electromagnetic, and Gravitational interactions are the respective 1D, 2D, 3D, and bulk volumetric geometric projections of this single invariant.

\item[Vacuum Damping Rate ($\Gamma_D$):] The constant visco-elastic phase lag that dissipates the energy of transverse optical waves propagating through the lattice. It is mechanically identical to the uncalibrated local Hubble Constant ($H_0$).

\item[Viscous Horizon ($R_U$):] The absolute optical boundary of the cosmos ($R_U = \frac{c_E}{\Gamma_D}$). It is the physical distance required for lattice drag to completely thermalize coherent transverse optical waves into the longitudinal phonon noise floor (the Cosmic Microwave Background).

\end{description}
