\chapter*{Table of Notation and Constants}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\protect\numberline{}Table of Notation and Constants}
\label{app:notation}

This appendix collects the fundamental constants, geometric boundaries, constitutive coefficients, and principal notation used throughout Tension Field Theory (TFT). Related symbols are grouped by physical role. Numerical values retain the manuscript's stated calibration or reference specialization; a mode-dependent coefficient is not an additional universal constant.

The surface coefficient $\tau_{\mathrm D}$, volumetric yield pressure $\tau_{\mathrm{D,bulk}}$, and mode-resolved stiffnesses are distinct quantities. Likewise, the structural order parameter $\Psi$ differs from the excitation field $\psi$, and the photon relaxation rate $\Gamma_{\mathrm D}$ differs from the structural growth rate $\Gamma_{\mathrm{pc}}$ and local receiving rates. Derivatives, component indices, and local comparison qualifiers belong to the same defined symbol family.
