# Tension Field Theory (TFT)

TFT is a foundational theoretical physics framework that replaces abstract expanding geometries and probabilistic wavefunctions with a rigorous, dual-scale mechanical continuum. 

This repository serves as the central manuscript and code hub for the Tension Field Theory research project.

## Abstract & Core Concepts
Tension Field Theory models the universe as a dual-scale, dual-phase Spacetime Gravitational Floor (SGF):
* **The Latent Substrate:** A phase-locked, simple-cubic (SC) mechanical reference providing absolute structural capacity.
* **The Active Vacuum:** A face-centered-cubic (FCC) nodal scaffold and interstitial condensate supporting finite-energy acoustic branches, electromagnetic correspondence, and localized matter.

By deriving physical observables from objective contact mechanics and coherent elastic constraints, TFT recovers the effective optical metric of gravity, the mechanics of quantum measurement resolution, and cosmological kinematics from first principles.
